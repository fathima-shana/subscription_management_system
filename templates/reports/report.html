How the figures are worked out

  • MRR: line amounts (quantity x price, before discounts and tax) of active subscriptions, turned into a month: yearly / 12, weekly x 52/12, daily x 365/12.
  • Invoiced: invoices issued in the period, without drafts and cancelled invoices.
  • Collected: payments dated in the period, without voided payments.
  • Outstanding and overdue: what is still owed on confirmed invoices today; overdue means the due date has passed.
